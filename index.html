How to Play

  • You are a spirit possessing Wobblesworth, the realm's drunkest wizard. He flops. A lot.
  • The Bar (hub): it's just a bar — wobble to the bar for tips, the 🚪 door to venture, or the 🪜 stairs up to your room. Press E (tap ✋) to interact.
  • Pick a stage at the door, then traverse its branching journey map (Slay-the-Spire-style): click glowing nodes to pick your path of ⚔️ skirmishes, 💀 elites, 💰 treasure, 🔥 campfires and 🛒 shops up to the 👑 boss. HP, level & gold carry between nodes.
  • Your room (up the stairs) starts bare but for a bed. Tap 🔨 Build to craft & place stations — Spell Table, Cauldron, Wardrobe, Anvil, Ledger, Quest Board — and comforts, then walk up to use them. Rest at the bed for an HP bonus.
  • Move: WASD/arrows or drag the left side on phone.
  • Cast: hold Right-Mouse (or draw on the right on phone) and trace a glyph. Cleaner = more damage; perfect = CRIT. Or tap a spell / press 1–3.
  • Mana = beer. It does NOT refill on its own — press Q (or tap 🍺) to drink. Each gulp tops up mana but makes the room spin (wonkier aim, woozy screen). Drink wisely!
  • Combos: draw two equipped glyphs in a row — e.g. △ then — = 🔥 Fire Tornado (learn them at the Cauldron).
  • Back at the tavern, work a bar shift (the Kitchen): accept orders and pour beer to the line for tips. Clear a stage for loot, then gear up!

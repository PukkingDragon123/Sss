WONKYWIZARD

A sloshed sorcerer. A meddling spirit. One very long night.

How to Play

  • You are a spirit possessing Wobblesworth, the realm's drunkest wizard. He flops. A lot.
  • The Bar (hub): wobble to the bar for tips, the 🚪 door to open the world map, or the 🪜 stairs up to your room. Press E (tap ✋) to interact.
  • The World Map: a 3D realm of island regions (monsters prowl it!). Tap a region to scout its danger & loot, then Venture in. Beat its boss to unlock the next region.
  • The Path (Slay-the-Spire style): after each step you pick a door — LEFT or RIGHT. Each previews what waits: ⚔️ a fight, 💀 an elite, 💰 treasure, 🔥 a campfire, ❓ a Mystery (choice event) or ✶ a Trial (skill challenge).
  • Abilities & Artifacts: level-ups, kegs, shrines & events grant abilities (they stack, top-left). Each region's crowned boss drops a build-defining ✦ artifact — collect them and carry up to 3 into future runs (Inventory). Every spell channels one of the 4 elements 🔥💧🌬️🪨.
  • Two purses: 💎 gems are won in battle — spend them on spells & research. 🪙 gold is earned only by working the bar. A day passes each shift or venture (the clock drives your research).
  • Cast: hold Right-Mouse (or draw on the right on phone) and trace a glyph. Cleaner = more damage; perfect = CRIT. Or tap a spell / press 1–3.
  • Mana = beer. It does NOT refill on its own — press Q (or tap 🍺) to chug: a 3-second drink (watch the bar) that fills mana to FULL. You can move while chugging, but it makes the room spin. Beer abilities change what a chug does.
  • Move: WASD/arrows or drag the left side on phone.
  • Your room (up the stairs) starts bare. Tap 🔨 Build to lay out your den, Clash-style — place stations (Spell Table [spends 💎], Arcane Library [📖 Grimoire / Research / Inventory], Cauldron, Equipment Hall, Anvil, Ledger, Quest Board) and comforts, then walk up to use them. Rest at the bed for an HP bonus.
  • Combos: draw two equipped glyphs in a row — e.g. △ then — = 🔥 Fire Tornado (learn them at the Cauldron).
  • Work the bar for gold: walk to a table to take an order, pour it at the bar, then carry it back to serve — bump a patron or table and you'll spill! Conquer regions for gems, artifacts & gear; forge gear at the Anvil; research at the Library.

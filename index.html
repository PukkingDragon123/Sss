The Spirit

✦ Drunken Epiphany ✦

The spirit grows stronger. Choose a boon:

WONKYWIZARD

A sloshed sorcerer. A meddling spirit. One very long night.

How to Play

  • You are a spirit possessing Wobblesworth, the realm's drunkest wizard. He flops. A lot.
  • Part 1 — The Tavern: steer the blind-drunk wizard to the glowing door. Try not to bump the patrons or send the furniture flying!
  • Part 2 — The Forest: you black out and wake in a moonlit wood, swarmed by goblins, bats, vampires, zombies and the Goblin King.
  • Move: WASD / arrows — or drag the left side on a phone (virtual stick).
  • Cast: hold Right-Mouse (or draw on the right side on phone) and sketch a glyph. Time slows while you draw. Or tap a spell / press 1–5.
  • Glyphs: △=Fireball · Z=Lightning · ◯=Frost Splash · V=Heal · —=Gust.
  • Soak up XP motes, level up, do forest chores, and slay the King to survive the night.

WONKYWIZARD

A sloshed sorcerer. A meddling spirit. One very long night.

How to Play

  • Move with WASD/arrows (drag the left side on phone). Cast by holding Right-Mouse (draw on the right on phone) and tracing a glyph — cleaner = more damage, perfect = CRIT. Or tap a spell / press 1–3.
  • Mana is beer and won't refill itself. Tap 🍺 (or Q) to chug: a 3-second drink fills mana to full — but spins the room.
  • The bar is your hub: the bar for work, the 🚪 door for the world map, the 🪜 stairs to your room. Press E / tap ✋ to interact.
  • Venture out: pick a region, then a door each step — ⚔️ fight, 💀 elite, 💰 treasure, ❓ mystery or ✶ trial. The last fork is the crowned boss, who drops a build-defining ✦ artifact (carry up to 3).
  • Two purses: 💎 gems (won venturing) buy spells & research; 🪙 gold (earned working & quests) builds your den.
  • Your room: tap 🔨 Build to place stations Clash-style — Spell Table, Library, Cauldron (combos), Equipment Hall, Anvil — then walk up to use them.
  • Work for gold: take a patron's order at a table, pour at the bar, carry it back to serve — don't spill!
